## About Us

Welcome to Signzy's document portal! Our platform redefines the speed, accuracy, and experience of customer and business onboarding for financial institutions using digital media.&#x20;

We are a market-leading platform that is revolutionizing the onboarding process for financial institutions using the digital medium. Our award-winning no-code GO platform delivers seamless, end-to-end, and multi-channel onboarding journeys while offering customizable workflows and access to a marketplace of 240+ bespoke APIs.

We work with over 240 financial institutions globally, including the 4 largest banks in India and a Top 3 acquiring Bank in the US. Our platform enables over 10 million end customer and business onboardings every month with a success rate of 99%, and we have reduced the time to market from 6 months to just 3-4 weeks for our clients.&#x20;
